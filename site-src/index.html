Your vehicle dates,
in one private place.

A personalized tracker for Android. Insurance, PUC, RC, service and licence dates, with a clear status for every vehicle and a reminder before anything expires.

What it does

Never miss a date

Phone reminders 30, 7 and 1 days before, and on the day.

Add a vehicle once

Insurance, PUC and PA cover link to it automatically.

Photograph your papers

Take a photo or choose a file and keep it with the record.

Your own categories

Track anything, such as house rent, with its own sheet.

Your data, your rules

Encrypted on your phone

Your records are encrypted on the device, and you can lock the app with your fingerprint or screen lock.

No ads, no selling

We do not sell your data and show no ads.

Optional Google Drive saving

If you sign in with Google, a copy goes to a folder in your own Drive. The app sees only files it creates.

Delete anytime

Remove any record, or your whole account, from inside the app.
