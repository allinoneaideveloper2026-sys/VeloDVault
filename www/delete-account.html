Delete your account and data

Last updated 8 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open VeloDVault and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete my account and data and type DELETE to confirm.

This removes your account, records, bin, feedback tickets and preferences from the device. Before deleting you can use Export my data.

Without the app

Email allinoneaideveloper2026@gmail.com from the address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We confirm and complete the request promptly, and within the time the law allows.

What is kept

Files stored in your own Google Drive stay there until you delete them in Drive. Feedback tickets already resolved are kept only as long as needed to resolve the issue and for record keeping, then deleted.
