Privacy notice

Last updated 8 November 2026

Who we are

VeloDVault ("we") is a personalized vehicle tracker operated by allinoneaideveloper2026. For privacy questions or complaints contact allinoneaideveloper2026, allinoneaideveloper2026@gmail.com.

What we handle

  • Your sign-in email address and an account identifier.
  • The vehicle records and documents you add. They are stored encrypted on your device and, if you sign in with Google in the Android app, in a folder named VeloDVault in your own Google Drive. The app can only see files it created (the Drive file permission). We do not keep a copy on our servers.
  • If you sign in with Google: your email address and name, used to identify your account.
  • Reminders: scheduled on your phone using notification permission; nothing is sent to us.
  • Camera: used only when you tap Take photo to capture a document. The photo stays on your device and, if you use Google sign-in, in your own Drive.
  • Messages you send us through Report / feedback (your message, your account email, ticket number and app version).
  • Anonymous usage totals, only if you opt in.

Why we use it

To sign you in, show your records and status, send reminders, and answer your feedback. We ask for your consent when you create an account. You may withdraw it at any time by deleting your account.

Sharing

We do not sell your data and show no ads. Service providers that process data for us: Google (sign-in, and your own Google Drive account if you use it) and our website hosting provider, Cloudflare. Some providers process data outside India.

How long we keep it

Account data until you delete your account. Feedback emails only as long as needed to resolve the issue, then they are deleted. Records on your device and in your Drive stay until you delete them.

Your rights

You can see, correct, export and delete your data inside the app (More). You may nominate another person to exercise your rights, and you can complain to us first and then to the Data Protection Board of India.

Children

VeloDVault is for people aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect data from children.

Security and breaches

We use encryption in transit, salted password hashing, input and file checks, and automatic sign-out. See the security page. If a breach affects your personal data we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.

Storage on your device

The app keeps its working data in your browser or device storage. It uses no advertising or tracking cookies.

Changes

We will show the date of any change at the top of this page.
