Security

Last updated 8 November 2026

How we protect your data

  • Records stay with you: on your device and in your own Google Drive.
  • Everything travels over HTTPS.
  • Passwords are salted and hashed; sign-in locks after repeated wrong tries.
  • Typed text is cleaned and uploaded files are checked (type, size and file signature) before use.
  • The app loads no third-party scripts or trackers, and a strict content policy limits where code can load from.
  • Automatic sign-out after 15 minutes of inactivity.
  • In the Android app: records are encrypted on the phone (AES-256) with a key kept in the Android Keystore; you can lock the app with fingerprint, face or screen lock; the screen is hidden in recent apps and screenshots are blocked in release builds; app data is excluded from cloud and device backups.
  • Reminders are scheduled on your phone; no reminder data is sent anywhere.

No service can promise to stop every attack. We review security every month and will commission an independent audit before the public release.
