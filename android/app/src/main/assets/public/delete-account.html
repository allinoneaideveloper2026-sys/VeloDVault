Delete your account and data

Last updated 3 November 2026

In the app

  1. Open VeloDVault and sign in.
  2. Go to More.
  3. Choose Delete my account and data and type DELETE to confirm.

This removes your account, records, bin, feedback tickets and preferences from the device. Before deleting you can use Export my data.
